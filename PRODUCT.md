# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16 App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4.

## Users

Primary: Adults (25-65) in Hyderabad seeking a trusted, premium dental clinic via Google search, word-of-mouth, or Google Maps. Secondary: Existing patients returning to book follow-up appointments.

## Product Purpose

SmileCraft Dental Studio is a premium private dental clinic in Banjara Hills, Hyderabad offering comprehensive dental care. The website converts a hesitant first-time visitor into a booked appointment by building immediate trust and making booking frictionless.

## Positioning

Premium private practice combining clinical excellence, modern technology, and calm patient experience -- the most trusted and design-forward dental clinic in Hyderabad.
